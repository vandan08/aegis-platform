Signed identity, verified at every hop, authorized by policy that fails closed. This is the whole system, and the next screen is a request moving through it.

Being inside the network buys nothing.

The gateway does not care where a request came from. It verifies the signature, reads the claims, and asks a policy engine whether this subject may do this thing to this resource. Then it decides.
